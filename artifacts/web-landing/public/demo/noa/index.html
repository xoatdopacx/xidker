03 / Behind the camera

Hello, I'm Noa.
Usually, I'm waiting.

For a cloud to move. For the street to empty. For the light to do that thing it did yesterday, when I didn't have my camera.

I'm a travel and landscape photographer based in the little harbour town of Ruunvik. I work with medium-format film, walk more than I need to, and make my prints in a darkroom that used to be a pantry.

I don't think a photograph has to say very much. Sometimes, it's enough to make you feel a little more here.
