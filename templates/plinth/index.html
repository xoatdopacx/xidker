04 / In the detail

Nothing extra.
Nothing overlooked.

A small palette, given room to speak. We care as much about the meeting of two materials as we do about the shape of a whole house.

01Timber

Warm to the touch. Honest about its age. We use timber where your hands meet the building, and let its grain do the talking.

02Lime plaster

A wall should be more than a flat colour. Hand-worked lime holds the trace of its making, softening the light as the day moves through a room.

03Stone

A cool floor in summer. A step worn smooth over time. We choose stone for the way it settles a building into its landscape, not just for its finish.

04Daylight

Our most changeable material. We draw a window for the view, but also for the patch of sun it places on the floor at four in the afternoon.

05 / A small, hands-on studio

Two partners. One drawing table.

A conversation,
from the beginning.

Plinth is Mara Ellery and Elias Rowan. We set up our studio in Aven Mere with a simple intention: to stay close to the work, and to the people we make it for.

One of us will be at the table from the first conversation to the final site visit. No handovers. Just a shared curiosity, a roll of tracing paper, and the time to get things right.
